Rebuild Data

Reads QuestionBank.xlsx directly in this browser — no server, no R, no internet connection — and regenerates the files the app uses.

Choose the workbook above to begin.

For the offline pendrive copy: the three files (questions.json, settings.json, data-embedded.js) go into this app's data/ folder, overwriting what's there. If your browser supports it, "Save to data/ folder" does this in one step — otherwise use the download button and move the three files into data/ yourself.

For the online (Netlify) deployment: commit and push questions.json and settings.json only — data-embedded.js is only used by the offline copy and should not be committed.

Keeping QuestionBank.xlsx up to date: if you edited or added questions above, download the updated workbook and save it over your working copy of QuestionBank.xlsx — that keeps it the source of truth for the next time you (or someone else) opens this tool.
